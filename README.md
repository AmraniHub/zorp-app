# Zorp

Download videos, MP3s and photos from YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Pinterest, X and
about 1,000 other sites, in one tap. Android 10 or newer (arm64 phones, which is almost all of them).

## Download

**[Download Zorp.apk](https://github.com/AmraniHub/zorp-app/releases/latest/download/Zorp.apk)** (75 MB)

The link always gives the newest version.

## Install

1. Open the downloaded **Zorp.apk**.
2. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to **install unknown apps**.
3. If Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap **More details > Install anyway**.
   Zorp is not on Google Play because Play does not allow video downloaders.
4. Open Zorp and allow notifications.

On Xiaomi phones, if it says "App not installed", turn on **Settings > Additional settings >
Developer options > Install via USB**, or install from the Mi file manager.

## Use

- **Share sheet**: in YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and the rest tap **Share > Zorp download**, then
  **Video**, **MP3** or **Image**.
- **Bubble**: turn it on in Zorp. Copy a link anywhere, tap the bubble, choose Video, MP3 or Image.
- **Quick Settings**: add the "Zorp link" and "Zorp bubble" tiles from Zorp > Settings.
- **Pause** any download and resume it later from where it stopped.
- **Whole playlists**: share a playlist and get every song as MP3, numbered, as one album.
- **WhatsApp Status**: save the statuses you have watched.

Quality defaults to the best: the highest video resolution (up to 4K), MP3 at 320 kbps with
title, artist and cover, TikTok without the watermark, photos at full size.

Files go to `Download/Zorp` (videos), `Music/Zorp` (songs) and `Pictures/Zorp` (photos).

When YouTube or TikTok stops working, open Zorp > Settings > **Update now**.

## Please note

Public videos and posts only, for your own use. Respect the creators and each site's terms.
Zorp never asks for your passwords and has no ads or tracking.
